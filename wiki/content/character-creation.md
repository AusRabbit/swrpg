New characters are built on the dashboard rather than on paper.

- The GM starts a new character on the GM page and sends you a private link.
- The link steps you through species, career and specialisation, characteristics, skills, talents, and your Motivation and Obligation. Your choices save as you go.
- When you're done, send it to the GM. Once they approve it, your character appears on the dashboard.

The species, career and specialisation lists come from the Star Wars RPG (FFG) community wiki, and each choice links to its full page there. Talents use this campaign's list: see [[talents]].
