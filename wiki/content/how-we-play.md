## Dice and checks

- Players roll their own checks, either with physical dice or the dashboard's Dice Roller. The GM rolls NPC checks openly.
- The difficulty of a check is stated before you roll, and it depends on the stakes rather than a fixed default.
- Declare what you want to do in your own words.
- Initiative slots aren't assigned to specific characters; anyone eligible on that side can take a slot.

## Destiny

- The Destiny pool is rolled at the start of each session.
- Spending a light side point turns it dark, and the other way round.

## Obligation

- Each character has an Obligation: something they owe, or something hanging over them.
- At the start of a session the GM rolls to see whether someone's Obligation comes up.

## Experience

- The GM awards XP at the end of a session. It appears on your character sheet straight away.
- Spend it from your sheet's Advancement Planner and press **Submit for approval**. The GM approves it on the GM page, and the new ranks or talents then appear on your sheet.
- Talents cost tier × 5 XP. Each tier must hold fewer talents than the tier below it.
